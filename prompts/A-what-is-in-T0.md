Ablation A: what is inside the fixed cost T0. This needs no GPU and runs on a laptop in a few minutes.

Read README.md, costmodel.py and the sections "Gates" and "Post hoc, not registered: T0 and the tail" of docs/exp01-results.md first. Write ablations/a_t0.py. Import from costmodel.py and do not modify it.

The question: the registered test failed on a T0 ratio of 2.20 on the local disk. How firm is that ratio, how much of T0 is the forward pass over the 16 tail tokens, and does putting that forward pass in the model improve prediction on a design held out of the fit?

Before any analysis, write ablations/A/decision_rule.json, save its SHA-256 to ablations/A/decision_rule.sha256, and have the analysis refuse to run if the file has changed. Include in it, in my words, what I expect to happen and why. State the comparison in Step 3 and what size of improvement over the sham would count, in percentage points of mean hit error. State plainly at the top that this is post hoc for Exp01, so a good result gives a hypothesis to test on new data, and no result here confirms or rescues the failed gate.

Run everything twice if data/pending_mla/ exists, once with the MLA rows and once without. If the folder is absent, run without, and use Qwen3-8B with FP8 as the third byte size in Steps 2 and 3, labelled as one design at two byte sizes.

Step 1. Put an interval on the ratio. For the local tier and for the Volume, resample prompts with replacement within each design and length, 2,000 times, with a fixed seed. In each resample compute the T0 ratio as costmodel.t0_ratio does, once with k fixed at the full-data joint fit and once with k refitted. Report the median, the 95% percentile interval and the share of resamples above 1.5 and above 2. Report the point estimate beside it, and explain any gap between the point estimate and the bootstrap median. On the Volume, say why T0 means little: the lab note explains this, and you should confirm it from the fit.

Step 2. Measure the tail. Mode `ctl_gpu` is a repeat of the same request with a GPU prefix-cache hit, so it has the same forward pass over the tail and no load. These rows exist at 4,096 and 32,000 tokens for GQA, GQA with FP8 and MLA, and not for MHA. Build T_tail(design, L) by linear interpolation in L between those two points on the local tier, and extrapolate below 4,096 without any clipping other than at zero, noting that this is an extrapolation. Report the table. For MHA, use GQA's T_tail as a stand-in and label it everywhere. Also report the results with MHA left out, so that a reader can see what the stand-in does.

Step 3. Refit and compare. Fit k and 1/B by non-negative least squares on relative error, on T_hit minus T_tail, leaving one design out as in costmodel.lodo. Predict the held-out design's hit time at each length as T_tail + k * chunks + L * b / B, using the held-out design's own measured T_tail. Compare with the registered model on mean absolute percentage error, per held-out design and overall. Then run the sham, which is the control a reviewer will ask for: give each held-out design another design's T_tail and refit. The tail term can be credited only with the improvement that the real tail shows beyond the sham. Also report the intercept left over per design when a free T0 is fitted on top of T_tail, and the ratio of those leftovers. Hold out the FP8 arm as well, since it has the same design at half the bytes.

Step 4. Draft the next test, and do not run it. Write ablations/A/next_test.md: which new design or designs would make a fair held-out test (use section 2.8 of docs/literature-review.md and check that a candidate needs no acceptance step), how many prompts per cell, which controls must run on every arm including MHA, and the gates as a reviewer would want them written before the run. Use the H100 container rate of $0.00137 per second from the lab note and the protocol times in docs/exp01-results.md to estimate the cost. The bill should stay small. State what result would stop the line of work.

Save results/ablations/A/table.csv, ratio_bootstrap.png (the distribution of the ratio with the 1.5 and 2 lines), tail_vs_length.png, heldout_error.png (registered, tail and sham, per held-out design) and results/ablations/A/NOTES.md. In the notes, give the verdict of my rule first, then the rest. The limits to state: at most five prompts per cell, two prompts in some MHA local cells, no measured tail for MHA, tail measured at two lengths on one server, and the same rows used to suggest and to check the idea. Do not modify data/. Keep all randomness seeded, and finish by printing the main table and three bullet points with the key numbers.
